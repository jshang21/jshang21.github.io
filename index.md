# Jing Shang

Hi, my name is Jing. I'm a third-year PhD student @[Stanford Statistics](https://statistics.stanford.edu/){:target="_blank"}. My research focuses on developing statistical learning methods that enable reliable decision-making from complex, high-dimensional data. I am particularly interested in combining statistical theory, machine learning, and optimization to build models that are both theoretically grounded and effective in real-world applications across economics, healthcare, and engineering.


Prior to Stanford, I received [Bachelor of Economics](https://econ.fudan.edu.cn/){:target="_blank"} from [Fudan Univerisity](https://www.fudan.edu.cn/){:target="_blank"} ('23).
